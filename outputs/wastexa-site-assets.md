## Concept

WasteXa keeps the current premium dark SaaS identity, but sharpens the symbol around the product job: waste streams moving through a coordinated circular workflow.

The outer form suggests circular-economy recovery and operational containment. The crossed center paths represent WasteXa Track, Cost and Sort: material data moving through classification, cost visibility and compliance decisions. Green carries recovery and operational improvement; amber marks cost/compliance signals inside the workflow.
